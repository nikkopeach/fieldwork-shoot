    02 / autofocus safety

    For all 11, keep the rows shallow. Move the wide AF area over the intended central face; if it chooses the wrong person, use Single-point AF near the middle depth. Review eyes across both rows at magnification. Check white clothing highlights without sacrificing face exposure. RAW gives you room to finish the color later.

    03 / your look in camera

    Open shade or soft cloud edge. Position faces toward unobstructed sky, keep the deeper trees behind, and preserve creamy skin highlights with dimension in the shadows. Avoid patchy noon sun, a flat reflector fill for the full group, or deliberately underexposing faces. Natural Light Auto is a daylight baseline, not a permanent warm grade. If every frame is in one unchanging light, a tested fixed white balance can improve consistency. Picture Control affects the preview/JPEG, not the finished Capture One RAW look. Keep your true mood in the light, relationships, composition and final grade.

    04 / the fuji + wider lens

    X100VI is optional for quiet environmental details and candid moments; preserve your existing C1–C7 recipes and shoot RAW. Nikon 16–35mm is an optional wider environmental lens at roughly 24–28mm—keep faces away from the frame edges and the camera level. The 35mm and 50mm primes can stay in the bag. Finish the 24–70mm essentials first.
